## Where the spec's estimates look wrong
- The total is about 55 hours of work against 34.5 on the clock. Sub-agents make Workstream B parallel, but integration into the app is serial and lands on one person.
- A is 16 h in the spec; I expect 21 to 23 because nothing in the packages has run in a browser yet (A0).
- B2 is more than 4 h: verifying an Intel TDX quote and an NVIDIA attestation in the client means either calling the vendors' verification services or checking certificate chains locally. I will verify what can be verified in the window and state exactly which checks ran.
- **T1 (8 h), Z1 and T2 will almost certainly be cut.** I am not scheduling them. If A1 to A5 and B1 to B4 are green by 02:00 UTC on Nov 14, T2 (auditor view) is the cheapest to add.
- Real-device passkey testing needs the owner; the automated test uses a virtual authenticator.
