# 13 Demo plan (about 3 minutes, two browsers side by side)

Principle: show outcomes, not machinery; every claim on screen has a See proof link. Mocks carry a visible tag.

## Acts and timing
| Time | Scene | What the viewer sees | What it proves |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:10 | Hook | "Ask for proof, not files." plus the redaction-bar motif | The idea |
| 0:10 to 0:40 | Keep and ask | Owner adds a synthetic lease and payslip; the box fills with a summary and index; owner asks "When does my lease end and what is the rent?" and gets a cited answer, with a "Private AI, attested" badge; click a citation to see the highlighted page | Private AI on the owner's own data; client-side encryption; OCR index |
| 0:40 to 1:10 | Answers (and redaction if built) | Owner approves an Answer "Income at least X: yes (Source: read from your file, reviewed)"; optionally a bank statement with AI-proposed redactions approved into a "Redacted for the landlord" copy | Claims instead of files; redaction parity |
| 1:10 to 1:50 | Give access | Owner gives the landlord access for 2 hours; the landlord opens the link on the right with no account and sees the Answer and the marked pages; the owner's Activity shows the receipt appear live; a quick tap on See proof shows the chain check | Receipt-first release; scoped access; watermark |
| 1:50 to 2:05 | Take back | Owner taps End access; the landlord refreshes and is refused | Revocation |
| 2:05 to 2:45 | Sale | A buyer sees the index and the Answers, pays the held payment, the waiting period timer runs (labelled as shortened), the owner cancels once and re-offers or lets it complete; the box and the payment move together; the buyer's app re-locks the box | Escrow, ownership transfer, key delivery |
| 2:45 to 3:00 | Close | Proof drawer: receipt chain verified, three transaction links, the honest-limits line, the track tags | Evidence |

## Priorities when time is short
Must: Keep and ask, Give access with receipt, Take back, Sale. Next: Answers. Then: Redaction. If the video must shrink, merge Answers into Give access and cut the redaction scene.

## Dependencies
The NEAR AI key (spike K7) for Ask my box and extraction; synthetic documents generated by the tools; a reset command; a fallback recording of the full run. If the key is not working, the Ask scene is shown from a labelled mock and the README says so.
