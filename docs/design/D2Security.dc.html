How Keyhole is checked

What we test, what a hostile review found and fixed, and what is still on the list.

Keys

  • Your keys are derived from your passkey each time you unlock. They are never written to disk.
  • Each shared key is split in two. One half travels in the link; the other is released after the visit is recorded.
  • Ending access stops a link at once. Handing over the safe changes its keys, so every old link stops working.
