[ Honest answers ]

Before you put anything in

Can someone screenshot it?

Yes. A photo of a screen cannot be blocked. Their name sits faintly across the page and the visit is logged.

Is a claim verified?

No. It is machine-read and reviewed by you, or self-declared. We never call it verified.

What does the AI see?

Only the lines you choose, encrypted to hardware that proves what it runs. Not that its answer is right.

Where does it run?

On NEAR testnet. The contract has no keys and its code is public, so anyone can check what it does.
